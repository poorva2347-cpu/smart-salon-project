About The Smart Salon

Why Smart Salon?

Running a salon can be confusing and stressful. Many salons still use old methods like phone calls, WhatsApp messages, or writing appointments in a diary. Because of this, appointments get mixed up, customers have to wait, and barbers face problems in managing their time.

Smart Salon is created to make everything simple.It helps customers book easily and helps barbers manage their schedule without any confusion.

We solve problems like:

Too many calls for booking

Barbers don’t know their schedule properly

No proper system to choose the preferred barber

Communication gaps between customer & barber
